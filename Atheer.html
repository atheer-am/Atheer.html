About the Platform

ProVocab is an interactive educational web app engineered to bridge the gap between computer science jargon and C1/C2 advanced English vocabulary.

Designed specifically for computer science students and developers, this platform utilizes bite-sized flashcards to deliver clear Arabic meanings, contextual definitions, direct antonyms, and instant native text-to-speech audio pronunciation.
